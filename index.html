About

Bharat Mavii

Born in the timeless city of Mathura and shaped by the chaos and colour of stories all around him, Bharat Mavii is a film director and writer who believes every frame should mean something. A graduate in Film Studies from Noida, he traded textbooks for shot lists early — and never looked back.

With a creative bent that blurs the line between raw emotion and visual poetry, Bharat has directed and written across films, shows, advertisements, and music videos. His body of work includes short films like The Pink Umbrella (2023) and Grey (2023), the evocative Outro (2024), ad campaigns for brands like Cadbury & Flipkart, London Beauty Academy, and Shakti Life Insurance, and music videos including Dil Tod Gi (2024) and O Sajana by Vishal Chhoker (2022).

Beyond the director's chair, Bharat leads production and post-production as Team Lead at The Art Circle Productions, Mumbai — where he orchestrates the invisible machinery that turns raw footage into finished cinema. For him, filmmaking isn't just a craft. It's a way of seeing the world.
