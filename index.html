About

Bharat Mavii

My journey began with studying filmmaking, where I built a strong foundation in both the creative and technical aspects of cinema. Theatre deepened my understanding of performance, helping me connect with actors, interpret scripts with greater sensitivity, and create a collaborative environment on set, while film workshops further refined my craft and expanded my creative perspective. I have worked across different areas of filmmaking, contributing as an Assistant Director and in Production on several short films, where I developed a strong appreciation for collaboration and discipline.

As a Director, I have led short films, music videos, and advertisements with a focus on compelling storytelling, visual clarity, and thoughtful execution. I enjoy leading creative teams, solving challenges on set, and bringing ideas to life with both vision and purpose. I am currently working at The Art Circle Productions, Mumbai.

This portfolio reflects my journey, my experiences, and my unwavering passion for cinema. Thank you for taking the time to explore my work.
